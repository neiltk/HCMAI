Our mission

Learn Hindustani classical music with clarity and structure.

HCMAI brings the discipline of Indian classical music into a modern digital experience. The platform combines thoughtful pedagogy, practical study tools, and a library designed to help learners understand raags, swaras, rhythm, and daily practice.

What we teach

  • Swara and note structure
  • Thaat and raag foundations
  • Taal and rhythmic understanding
  • Riyaaz and daily practice routines
  • Bandish creation and notation

Why it matters

Music learning becomes more consistent when it is guided, structured, and easy to revisit. HCMAI turns complex concepts into actionable learning journeys that fit modern routines.
