Thaat Explorer

Bilawal Natural and bright

All notes are shuddha, creating a clear and uplifting tonal character.

  • Key raags: Des, Kedar, Bilawal
  • Typical mood: serene and open
Kalyan Royal and expansive

Contains the tivra Madhyam and establishes a majestic, full-bodied melody.

  • Key raags: Yaman, Kalyan, Miyan ki Malhar
  • Typical mood: grand and devotional
Khamaj Soft and melodic

Often associated with a gentle emotional flow and a relaxed tonal balance.

  • Key raags: Kamod, Bageshree, Gaud Sarang
  • Typical mood: lyrical and romantic
Bhairav Devotional and intimate

A highly expressive thaat used for emotional and spiritual depth.

  • Key raags: Bhairav, Ahir Bhairav
  • Typical mood: contemplative and warm
Asavari Reflective and tender

Known for a gentle, moving melodic quality with emotional nuance.

  • Key raags: Asavari, Darbari Kanada
  • Typical mood: introspective and graceful
Bhairavi Dark and dramatic

Creates weight, contrast, and intensity through a more grounded tonal spectrum.

  • Key raags: Malkauns, Bhairavi
  • Typical mood: deep and powerful
